From signed agreement to first pass in 3 steps

  1. 1 / 3

    Agreement and security review

    Sign the service agreement and complete our security review. Export-controlled missions get an additional compliance check.

  2. 2 / 3

    Compatibility review

    Share your telemetry and command dictionary. Standard formats connect directly; custom protocols go through an integration engagement.

  3. 3 / 3

    Rehearsal and go-live

    We configure your console, book ground station passes and rehearse with your operators. Then you fly.
